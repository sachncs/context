# Roadmap

Built and shipped in 0.1.0: see the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Next

* Long-term conversation memory: summaries and recall on top of the notes stores.
* Table-aware PDF extraction.
* Streaming `ask`.
* Support-level grading of claims against cited text (beyond quote presence).

## Considered, not started

* Vector database connectors.
* Multimodal pages (vision) and OCR for scanned documents.
* PII redaction before text leaves the machine.
* A TypeScript SDK.
* A hosted evaluation dashboard.

Open an issue to vote or to propose something else.
